Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my order?

After placing your order you'll see an Order ID and a live status screen. Once an admin updates the status, your screen refreshes automatically — no need to reload!

What is the minimum order value for free delivery?

Free delivery on all orders above ₹300. For orders below ₹300, a nominal ₹30 delivery charge applies.

Can I modify my order after placing it?

Call us within 5 minutes of placing the order and we'll do our best. Once the order status shows "Preparing" we're unable to make changes.

Do you cater for large events or office parties?

Absolutely! Email us at catering@spicefusion.in with your requirements at least 48 hours in advance and we'll build you a custom menu and quote.
