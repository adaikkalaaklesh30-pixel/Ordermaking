From a Home Kitchen to Your Doorstep

SpiceFusion was born in a modest kitchen in Tiruppur in 2018, when childhood friends Arjun Narayanan and Chef Meera Krishnan decided the city deserved authentic, soul-warming food that didn't require dressing up or waiting an hour.

What started as weekend meal kits delivered by bicycle grew into a full-scale cloud kitchen within 18 months, fuelled by word-of-mouth and Meera's legendary Chicken Biryani recipe — passed down three generations from her grandmother's kitchen in Madurai.

Today, SpiceFusion operates from two state-of-the-art cloud kitchens, employs over 40 passionate food professionals, and delivers more than 800 orders every single day across Tiruppur and Coimbatore.
